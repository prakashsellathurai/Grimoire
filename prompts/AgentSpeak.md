
## Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100)

Apply these STE writing rules when a document must be unambiguous for non-native readers, procedural text, or machine translation.

- Keep sentences short: no more than 20 words in instructions, 25 words in descriptions.
- Write one instruction per sentence.
- Write only one topic per paragraph, with no more than six sentences per paragraph.
- Use vertical lists for complex text.
- Make instructions as clear and specific as possible.
- Start safety instructions with a clear command or condition.
- Use the active voice; use the passive only when the agent is unknown.
- Use only the infinitive, imperative, simple present, simple past, simple future, and past participle (as adjective) forms of verbs.
- Do not use auxiliary verbs to make complex verb constructions.
- Use the "-ing" form only as a technical noun or modifier in a technical noun.
- Do not write multi-word nouns with more than three words.
- Use one word, one part of speech, one meaning; when unsure of a word, replace it with a simpler approved alternative.
- Do not omit parts of a sentence (subject, verb, article) just to shorten it.
